About

Four years of design that works as hard as you do.

I’m Fisayo, the designer behind Fisayo Grafix. For over 4 years I have helped businesses, schools, churches, event organisers and growing brands show up with confidence, from a single flyer to a full month of social media content.

Every project starts with a simple question: what should people think, feel or do when they see this? From there I choose the layout, colours and type that answer it clearly, then polish every detail until it is ready to post or print.

My work has covered fintech app adverts, sports fixtures, school admissions, university events, professional invitations and seasonal greetings. I’m comfortable moving between bold and playful, and clean and corporate.
